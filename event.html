Community Spotlight: Transformers Festival

This Transformes event will take place on Saturday from 12pm - 8pm. The festival is a celebration of the 42th anniversary of the Transformers franchise. There will be all your favorite characters and activities.

Featured Activities

  1. Autobot Parade
  2. Decepticon Showdown
  3. Transformers Trivia Contest
  4. Cosplay Competition
  5. Merchandise Booths

Attendee Tips

Arrive early to secure a good spot for the parade.

Bring your own snacks and water, as food vendors may have long lines.

Don't forget to bring your camera for photos with your favorite characters!

Check the weather forecast and dress accordingly.

Attendee Testimony

"I had an amazing time at the Transformers Festival! The parade was incredible, and I loved participating in the trivia contest. Can't wait for next year!" - Mariono D.
